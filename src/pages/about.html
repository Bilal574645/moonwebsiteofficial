Our story

Built by Kahuta, for Kahuta's children

Moon Light Islamic Public School was founded to give families in Kahuta Town a place where academic learning and Islamic values grow side by side. What started as a small neighbourhood school on Ghori Road has become a place where students move confidently from their first alphabet to their Class 8 board preparation.

We keep our classes close-knit on purpose. Teachers know each student's name, their strengths, and where they need a little more time — because in a small school, no child is just a number on a register.

What guides us

Four things every classroom is built on

Faith

Islamic Studies and daily practice woven into the rhythm of the school day, not set apart from it.

Knowledge

A strong grounding in Mathematics, Science and English that prepares students for what comes next.

Discipline

Clear routines and respectful conduct that give children the structure to focus and grow.

Community

A single campus where parents, teachers and students in Kahuta Town know one another by name.

Every child who walks through our gate on Ghori Road brings a family's hopes with them. Our job is simple to say and hard to do well: teach them to read, to reason, and to carry their faith with confidence into whatever they choose next.

How the school has grown
